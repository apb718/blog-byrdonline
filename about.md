I'm Alec — I work on infrastructure, Linux, and the assorted plumbing that keeps systems running. This site is where I park things I want to remember and occasionally write up more carefully.

## What you'll find here

- **Writing** — longer posts on Ansible, RHEL, HPC, homelab projects, and whatever rabbit hole I'm currently down.
- **Notes** — shorter, less-polished pieces. Commands, gotchas, small patterns.

## Topics I reach for often

- Ansible and the `rhel-system-roles` collection
- SELinux, systemd, and the RHEL side of things generally
- HPC scheduling and cluster ergonomics
- Homelab tinkering — networking, storage, virtualization

## Colophon

Built with [Jekyll](https://jekyllrb.com/). Source is kept deliberately simple — a handful of layouts, SCSS partials, and Markdown posts. No tracking, no ads.
